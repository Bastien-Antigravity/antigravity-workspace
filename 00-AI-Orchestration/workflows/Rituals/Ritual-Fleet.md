---# 🛰️ Fleet-Commander Rituals (Level 3)

These rules are MANDATORY when operating in **Mode 3**.

## 1. The Integrity Loop (Fleet-Scale)
When a fleet action is complete, you must:
1. **Sync READMEs**: Update every microservice `README.md` impacted by the action.
2. **Cross-Repo Update**: Update the `Ecosystem-Map-MOC` or Architecture nodes if a system-wide rule was changed.
3. **Deployment Log**: Write a formal entry in `05-Fleet-Operation/Deployment-Logs/`.

## 2. Multi-Repo Sovereignty
- Ensure all changes are committed across all impacted repositories simultaneously to prevent "Desync Drift."
